## Two statements that must appear in the paper (agreed with Person 3)
1. Operation counts are approximate scalar-operation estimates (add/mul/compare = 1, transcendental = cx), not measured hardware
   instructions and not energy consumption. cx is varied 3 / 10 / 25 consistently in BP and in the score.
2. Thresholds are selected to MINIMISE MEAN ITERATIONS under the excess-event budget. Operation savings are supplementary results,
   not the optimisation objective.

## Headline numbers
- Matched calibration (region: syndrome > 0), q_now: AUC 0.967-0.971 (raw 0.939-0.947); q_fail: AUC 0.92-0.95, PR-AUC 0.91 / 0.88 / 0.87 / 0.82
  (2.00 to 2.75 dB), slope 0.98-1.05. q_fail intercept drifts with SNR (+0.41 at 2.00 dB to -0.59 at 2.75 dB): a pooled model
  under-states failure risk at low SNR and over-states it at high SNR.
- Mismatch, q_now ECE at 2.25 dB: raw 0.012 (delta 0) -> 0.107 (delta -3); matched calibrators 0.0045 -> 0.0105 (-3), 0.0197 (+3);
  refit calibrators stay at 0.0034-0.0057 for every delta. q_fail at 2.25 dB: matched 0.013 (delta 0) -> 0.058 (+3); refit 0.012-0.022.
- Under mismatch the refit calibrators restore budget compliance at 2.25 dB, delta = -3 (74 -> 31 excess events, budget 62.6).

## Cautions to state
- Budget compliance on test: thresholds were tuned to the edge of the budget on validation, so some rules exceed it on test:
  P-cal both 1 of the 8 cells at rho 10% and 20% (2.00 dB, 10%: 69 vs 66.7 events), B5 4 of 8 (e.g. 2.75 dB, 10%: 38 vs 28.1). `p2_paper_tables.py` also counts the 5% cells. Compared rules are therefore not
  always at an equal realised budget; report the realised excess next to every saving.
- Fragility: the 5% budget at 2.75 dB is flagged (7.7 validation events). The 2.75 dB test split has 281 failing frames.
- Accepting early raises the undetected-error rate among accepted frames: for P-cal accept-only at 2.00 dB it is 0.7% (10% budget) and 1.6% (20% budget), smaller at higher SNR (about 0 at 2.75 dB), against about 0.1% for syndrome-zero.
- The accept head cannot be trusted below q about 0.01 (confident-but-wrong frames put a floor of a few percent under any posterior score).
- One code family (5G NR BG2, K = 110, N = 220), AWGN/BPSK, SNR 2.00-2.75 dB, 50 BP iterations. Mismatch covers the assumed noise variance
  only. The refit analysis is at one SNR (2.25 dB). Mismatch-test frames are the same frames as the matched test (paired, not independent).
- Not claimed: posterior-based early stopping as a new idea, certified guarantees, energy savings.

## Scope of what could still be done (NOT done; do not claim)
SNR-aware calibration (to remove the q_fail intercept drift); tuning thresholds for total operations; cheaper score approximations or a
sparser evaluation schedule; a safety margin on the validation budget.
